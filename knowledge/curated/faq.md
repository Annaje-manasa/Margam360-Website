# Frequently asked questions

## How do I get started with Margam360?
Book the free 30-minute growth call (the "Book a call" button on the website), message on WhatsApp at +91 88855 54621, or email hi@margam360.com. Before the call the team reviews your current marketing (Brand Audit Snapshot), and after it you keep a free content calendar and a custom 90-day growth roadmap.

## Is the strategy call really free? Is there any obligation?
Yes. The 30-minute growth call is free, with no pitch and no pressure, and you keep the plan either way.

## How much do your services cost? What is your pricing?
Pricing is not published on the website. Engagements are scoped to each brand's goals and budget, so the best way to get a number is the free strategy call, or by emailing hi@margam360.com.

## Where is Margam360 located? Do you work with clients outside Hyderabad?
The office is at Madhusha Enclave, Beside NGRI Metro Station, Street No. 5, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Margam360 works with brands across India and the US.

## What are your working hours?
Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST.

## What kinds of businesses do you work with?
The published client stories include an e-commerce fashion & lifestyle brand in Hyderabad and a B2B SaaS startup in Telangana. Margam360 also turns down clients it cannot genuinely help ("Zero Junk Business"), so the strategy call is where fit is decided.

## How do you report results?
Margam360 defines measurable KPIs before spending begins, optimises every week, and provides transparent reporting.

## Will I have a dedicated team?
Yes. Margam360 runs small, dedicated growth teams with no account handoffs.
